## 📌 About

**Awesome Telegram AI Bots** collects the best **AI bots on Telegram**: AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Grok and more), AI image generation, AI translation and language learning, and AI assistants / voice / music.

How bots get listed:

- **Verified one by one**: every bot's [t.me](https://t.me) profile was opened and checked — it must be an actual bot (with a Start Bot entry), and its operator and feature description must match the profile. Dead bots, missing profiles and name-only entries are not listed
- **Official vs. third-party is labeled**: we never invent an "official" label (Midjourney, for example, has no official Telegram bot — everything you see is third-party)
- **Monthly users shown when available**: if a profile displays its monthly active users, we record it as a popularity reference
- **Last verified: 2026-10-31**

Looking for other categories (group management, resource search, downloads, channel tools…)? See the master list: **[Awesome Telegram Bots CN](https://github.com/Armorys/awesome-telegram-bots-cn/blob/main/README.en.md)** (web version: https://armorys.github.io/awesome-telegram-bots-cn/en.html).

## How to Tell If an AI Bot Is Legit

- **Know who is actually "official"**: OpenAI has **no** official Telegram bot — ChatGPT's official homes are its website and apps. DeepSeek, Claude and Gemini have no official bots either; anything claiming otherwise is a third-party wrapper. Bots labeled "official" in this list (Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity) have ✔-verified profiles; treat everything else as third-party.
- **Try small before you pay**: aggregator bots usually run on a free quota plus paid plans. Use up the free quota first, check the quality, and never prepay a large amount or a long subscription up front.
- **Don't share sensitive data**: never send passwords, one-time codes, bank details or private documents to a bot. Photos and files you upload land on a third party's servers.
- **Double-check the username**: popular bots have look-alike clones (an extra underscore, one swapped letter). Entering through the links in this list is the safest route — and re-check the spelling before you pay for anything.

## FAQ

**Q: Is there an official ChatGPT bot on Telegram?**
A: No. OpenAI has never released an official Telegram bot; every "ChatGPT" bot is a third-party integration. Microsoft Copilot (@CopilotOfficialBot) and Perplexity (@askplexbot) in this list *are* official bots from their respective companies, with ✔-verified profiles.

**Q: Which bot should I use for free AI images?**
A: Start with @AIImageBot or @stable_diffusion_bot for free generations; try @midjourneyfreebot (third-party) for a Midjourney-style look with a free quota; the aggregators @GPT4Telegrambot and @syntxaibot also include free quotas — check the current rules inside each bot.

**Q: Do AI bots cost money?**
A: Most are freemium: a free quota covers light use, while the newest models, HD images and long conversations usually need a subscription or credits. Always try small first.

**Q: How do I add an AI bot to a group?**
A: Open the group's settings, choose "Add member" and add the bot by username. Translators like @TransChat_bot and @TgTranslatorBot start working after a quick in-group setup; inline bots like @dalle_query_bot can be invoked in any chat by typing @username. Step-by-step: [How to Add a Bot to a Group](https://github.com/Armorys/awesome-telegram-bots-cn/blob/main/README.en.md#how-to-add-a-bot-to-a-group) in the master list.

**Q: Can I trust what AI bots tell me?**
A: Not blindly. AI models hallucinate — they state made-up facts with confidence. Verify anything important against the original source, and prefer web-connected bots like @askplexbot for fact-checking questions.
